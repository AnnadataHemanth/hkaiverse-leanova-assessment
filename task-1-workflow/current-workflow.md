## Workflow hypotheses to investigate

1. A placement opportunity is received and reviewed.
2. An authorized person verifies the opportunity details and criteria.
3. The opportunity is communicated to the intended students.
4. Students review eligibility and application instructions.
5. Students complete the required application.
6. Questions, corrections and exceptions are handled.
7. Relevant application progress is reviewed where records are available.

## Roles to validate
- Placement administrator or placement cell
- Students
- Recruiter or employer, where applicable
- Technical support or system administrator, where applicable

## Handoffs and approvals to investigate
- Opportunity receipt to verification
- Verification to publication approval
- Publication to student communication
- Student queries to the responsible person
- Corrections and cancellations to updated communication
- Failures to resolution or escalation
