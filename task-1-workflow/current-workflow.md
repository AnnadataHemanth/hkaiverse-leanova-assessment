## 2. Current Process

Based on the student's account, the preliminary process is as follows:

1. **Company outreach:** A company shares job details and an application link with the placement cell.
2. **Announcement:** The placement cell distributes the opportunity details through email and WhatsApp.
3. **Student review:** Students read the opportunity details and review the stated eligibility criteria.
4. **External application:** Students use the supplied link to apply through the relevant external application platform.
5. **Student communication:** Students may need to contact the placement cell for clarification. The actual query-handling process has not been verified.
6. **Application tracking:** The method used to confirm external submissions and calculate the number of completed applications remains unknown.

This sequence is a preliminary process hypothesis. The order, responsible roles, approval steps, and exceptions must be validated before the workflow can be treated as an accurate current-state map.

## 4. Potential Operational Problems

Based on the student's account, the following issues are worth investigating:

1. **Fragmented communication:** Opportunity details are distributed through more than one channel, potentially making it harder to identify the latest information.
2. **Uncentralized queries:** Students may lack a single, clearly defined place to submit questions and follow up on answers.
3. **Uncertain application counts:** A link click does not establish that an application was completed. Without reliable confirmation, actual submissions may be difficult to count.
4. **Opportunity changes:** Updated eligibility criteria, deadlines, or application links may need to be communicated across multiple channels.
5. **Potential rework:** Repeated questions, corrections, or follow-ups may consume staff time, but the frequency and effort have not been measured.

These are potential problems, not confirmed institutional failures. Their frequency, severity, and operational impact remain unknown.
