# Proposed Workflow — PlacementHub

## Objective
Evaluate PlacementHub as a proposed way to streamline college placement
coordination while preserving accurate information, eligibility checks,
authorization and human oversight.

## Developer-reported capabilities
- Administrator functionality
- Placement announcements
- Eligibility rules
- In-app student notifications
- Unread notification status
- Application-related functionality
- Audit-related functionality

These features have been reported by the developer and have not yet
been independently verified for this assessment.

## Proposed workflow
1. An authorized administrator enters placement details.
2. Eligibility criteria and application instructions are reviewed.
3. Required information is checked before publication.
4. The authorized administrator publishes the opportunity.
5. Students access the opportunity and supported in-app notifications.
6. Students follow the stated application process.
7. Available application status and activity information is reviewed.
8. Administrators investigate incomplete information and exceptions.
9. Relevant activity records are reviewed where the audit functionality
   supports the investigation.

## Potential benefits to investigate
- Less repeated communication
- Easier access to eligibility criteria
- Improved visibility of unread announcements
- Easier access to application instructions
- Better traceability of administrative activity

These are hypotheses, not measured benefits. Comparison requires a
validated baseline for the college's existing workflow.

## Limitations and verification
Do not assume SMS or email delivery, guaranteed notification delivery,
complete audit coverage, or duplicate prevention without evidence.
Human review remains necessary for consequential placement decisions.

Evaluate the existing application with synthetic data in an authorized,
isolated environment. Do not modify the production app.
